Assault on Giant Mountain
The human city of Entridge is under siege. Ancient giants have returned, driving gnaths, minotaurs and ogres before them, while the dwarves hide in their caves and the elves have all but vanished. With refugees pouring in and the king's own outpost gone silent, only a daring assault on the giants' mountain fortresses can save the city.

Castle in the Clouds
You came to McBane City for a little rest, but while you traveled, evil overran the land, and the great wizard Zukar, its age-old protector, has vanished from his castle in the clouds. Find Zukar and turn back the rising tide of darkness. Part one of a two-part tale that concludes in White Dragon, and a fine next step after City of Bywater.

City of Bywater
Bywater is an honest city under a kind king, yet a cult of spider worshipers has raised a tower just east of its walls, goblins roam the southern ranges, and orc tribes prowl the east. Rather than one grand quest, it offers a string of smaller adventures, making it the ideal place to start a new party and learn the ways of the Realmz.

Destroy the Necronomicon
After centuries of hard-won peace in the kingdom of Nador, a strange tower appears on an island near the capital, Westmoore, and a portal on its side spills demons into the streets. Then a second tower rises, and a third, and a fourth. Discover what these bastions are, destroy them, and drive their demon hordes back to the hells that spawned them.

Griloch's Revenge
Four hundred years ago, the Berhune clerics gave their lives to forge the Spear of Light and drove the mad god Griloch from their land, and he swore to return when they were most at peace. Now his cults are raiding the outlying towns once more, and the Spear has been lost for ages. Find it and destroy Griloch once and for all in the second and final chapter of the story begun in Prelude to Pestilence.

Half Truth
Shipwrecked and stripped of everything you carried, you wash ashore on the storm-battered coast of Karnak, a once-proud nation worn down by generations of invasion. Strange forces roam its weakened lands, and nobody you meet is telling you the whole story. Survive, and untangle the web of lies and half-truths that has consumed this place.

Mithril Vault
The dwarves of Silverpeak Mountain have stopped sending mithril to the town of Winterhaven, and now they turn away anyone who approaches their warrens. Descend into the mountain to learn why, and confront your old nemesis Morbius, the lich who has nearly killed you twice before. Beasts from beyond the stars and mechanical giants the size of an inn stand in your way.

Prelude to Pestilence
The small, peaceful town of Mountain View has been violated by an unknown band of evildoers. Join forces with Mayor Hodar and his allies to discover who is behind the attacks, and drive the invaders out. This is the first chapter of the story of Griloch, which concludes in Griloch's Revenge.

Trouble in the Sword Lands
After weeks crossing the Khanark Rockies, you arrive in the Sword Lands, a lawless coast of merchant lords, warlords and fugitives, sent by the King of Bywater to learn who is disrupting its trade. The trail leads to the Overlords, a shadowy cabal plotting to conquer the Realmz. Part one of the Sword Lands trilogy, for characters of about 5th level.

Twin Sands of Time
The heroes of old have retired, and a new generation of untested youngsters answers a plea from across the Gargonian Sea, where orcs and goblins grew strangely organized the very night the land's twin heirs vanished. Start a brand-new party of six 1st- or 2nd-level characters, fight your way through more than 200 battles, and bring the twins home.

War in the Sword Lands
The ruthless Warlord of Sharranth has taken Sparklestone and laid siege to Wailsrond, and his mercenary army is sweeping across the Sword Lands. Survive a great war of brute force and treachery, and foil the six remaining Overlords before they bring back the god-like Mind Lords. Part two of the Sword Lands trilogy, for characters of 12th level and up.

White Dragon
At the great wizard Zukar's request, you have come to the outskirts of Brierwood in search of the fabled White Dragon, only to find the city's lord ensnared by the sorcerer Zaphres. Finding the dragon may be your only hope of defeating your greatest foe. The sequel to Castle in the Clouds.

Wrath of the Mind Lords
Born into bondage, you toil in the mines of the Neo-Imperium of Lankharh, ruled for six centuries by five immortal psionic god-emperors, the Mind Lords. The slaves still whisper of Destined Ones who will rise to destroy them. Unravel a conspiracy spanning centuries and worlds to free the Sword Lands in the third and final part of the Sword Lands trilogy.

Araman's Ring
You have wandered into a miserable little mountain village, and someone has stolen the Ring of Araman, the only profit from your last adventure. Everyone looks pathetically average and innocent, and the gates are locked tight. Stuck in this rathole until you get your ring back, you had better start asking questions.

City of Port Hyrtin
The Kingdom of Hyrtin is crumbling: the army still holds the capital, but red dragons and trolls ravage the outlying villages, Princess Emma has been kidnapped, and the legendary sword Alexander has disappeared. Rescue the princess, recover the sword and four magical stones, and destroy the evil at the heart of it all. A very high-level adventure, continued in Dagger of Shine.

Dagger of Shine
Queen Emma has sent you to her kingdom's eastern frontier, where a village girl has vanished, likely into the hands of the pirates who have turned the once-thriving port of Salevo into a den of crime. Storm their stronghold to save her, and you will find her plight is only the first thread in a far darker tapestry. The sequel to City of Port Hyrtin.

Dead of Night
Weary from the road, you arrive at the gates of the great city of Southgate just as the Times of Darkness foretold by the seer Zoniax begin. Will you stop the darkness, or be swallowed by it forever? Book One of the Prince of Death Cycle, a huge and brutally challenging adventure for a party of around 22nd level, continued in Dragon of Death.

Dragon of Death
The avatar of the dark goddess Shades is loose along the Middle Azure Coast, working to bring the avatar of Domorash into the world. Can you stand against two evil goddesses at once? Book Two of the Prince of Death Cycle, following Dead of Night, and a huge, brutally challenging adventure for a party of around 25th level.

Elemental Strife
Crosswald was once a thriving city where the followers of every element traded in peace, until the four Elemental Lords walled off a quarter each and plunged their domains into war. Carry the old mayor's plea to each Lord and bring all four together in Crosswald before the strife destroys the Realmz. Part one of the Elemental trilogy, continued in Journey into the Mire.

Hax
Somewhere beyond the Madwood, a haunted, half-flooded valley of enraged willows and crazed beasts, lies the elusive town of Hax, said to overflow with gems of tremendous power. Many have tried to open a road to it and found only an unmarked grave. Unravel the secrets of the Madwood, where power hides behind the mask of weakness and the armor on your back may seal your doom.

Journey into the Mire
With the Elemental Lords united and Crosswald booming, you are an honored citizen in an age of peace, until the Domain of Fire slides toward civil war. Save it, learn the dark origins of Chromos the chromatic dragon, and venture deep into the swamps to prepare for a final siege against him. Part two of the Elemental trilogy, following Elemental Strife.

Kalypso's Island
A storm tears apart your ship on the way to the court of Bywater, and after days clinging to its broken mast, you wash up on a great, rock-bound island. With little more than your wits and a few coins, you must survive, find out where you are, and learn what this perilous island is hiding. Part I of the Princess Aria Collection.

Lachis
Sandstorms batter your home village of Jakt, and something far worse now threatens its very existence. Set out on an epic quest across three lands to save your small corner of the world, and watch that world change with every choice you make.

Search for the Lost City
King Tial of Cestra needs heroes to destroy the Dreggs, a cult of evil mages whose raids grow bloodier by the week. Only the Abyssmal Wrath can destroy their head mage, and it lies in the city of Caelum, which vanished from the face of the world 156 years ago. Find the lost city, then carry the weapon deep into the Dreggs' own lands.

Spires of Steel
Sent to hunt orcs on a forgotten island, you instead meet Ranloth, a god who warns of beings from beyond the stars bearing weapons beyond magic, and he sends you forward with four words: seek the spires of steel. A blinding light drops you into Central City, a rusted, neon-lit future of blasters, gangs, killer robots and an armored police state, where magic means nothing.

Trial by Fire
When the gods tire of a mortal, they cast them into the Oobleck, the forgotten prison-world where they hide their mistakes, and that is where you now find yourselves. Among immortal exiles and dragons, your only way out is to wake the fabled Dragon Child and face Damarias, the insatiable beast the gods once used to devour their mistakes.
